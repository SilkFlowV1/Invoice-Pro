FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Invoice Pro is a single HTML file. Just double-click it — it opens in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). No installation, no account, no internet needed.

Will it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes! It's fully responsive and works beautifully on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. You can create and send invoices from anywhere.

Can I customize the colors to match my brand?

Absolutely. Choose from 12 preset palettes or use the color picker to enter any hex color. The entire invoice updates live, including accents, tables, and stamps.

How does the PDF export work?

Click "Save as PDF / Print". Your browser's print dialog opens. Choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. The invoice is already formatted for US Letter / A4 — perfect one-page output.

Can I reuse invoices for repeat clients?

Yes. Use the Save button (JSON format) to store any invoice. Later, click Load to bring it back — all fields, logo, signature, and settings restored.

Yes. Pay $19 once. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no upsells. You get the file and all future updates for life.

Yes. Use it for your own business, freelance work, agency, or clients. You can't resell or redistribute the file itself, but you can use it to bill as much as you want.
